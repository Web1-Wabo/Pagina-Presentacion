# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no frameworks or external libraries. Published on GitHub Pages under `/pagina-personal/` through GitHub Actions; tests with Node.js 22 (`node:test`) and html-validate.

## Users

Two audiences, both confirmed: the DAW teaching staff who evaluate the page as a course deliverable, and companies looking for work-placement (FCT) students who read it as a real portfolio. Both scan quickly for who Alvaro is, what he studies, what he can do and where his code lives.

## Product Purpose

Personal page of Alvaro Sanchez Segura, a DAW student: present profile, training, skills and three public GitHub repositories, and make contact (email, GitHub) one step away. Success: a reviewer understands the profile in under a minute and reaches a repository or the email without searching.

## Brand Commitments

- Tone: sober and professional (confirmed).
- Binding visual constraints given by the user: palette `--color-primario #2563EB`, `--color-secundario #0F172A`, `--color-fondo #F8FAFC`, `--color-texto #1E293B`, `--color-acento #F59E0B`; Georgia for headings, system-ui for text, no downloaded fonts; header with avatar left, name centered, menu centered below; 3-column project grid (1 column at 768px); 8px radius with shadows; dark mode via `prefers-color-scheme`.

## Evidence on Hand

Real content only: name, headline, about text, two training entries (SMR at IES San Juan Bosco, DAW at IES Ramón Arcas Meca), skills list, three repositories (php-words, ejerciciosPHP, practica_cicd). No testimonials, metrics, employers or photos exist; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Content first: every block answers a reviewer's question.
- Accessible by default: the headline promises accessible web development, so the page itself must prove it.
- Nothing invented: only facts the user supplied.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in light and dark modes, keyboard navigable, skip link, reduced-motion respected.
